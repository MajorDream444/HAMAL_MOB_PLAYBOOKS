# Marketing Asset Packager

Read [production-contract.md](references/production-contract.md) before production.

Version: 0.1.0

## Workflow

1. Start from an approved master and campaign message. Record channel, target audience, crop, dimensions, copy and call to action for each deliverable; confirm current platform constraints when necessary.

2. Produce thumbnails, store images, social crops and video backdrops from reusable components. Recompose intelligently; never stretch the logo or crop critical title/face details.

3. Keep headlines editable and check at thumbnail scale. Preserve exact logo, visual family and reference portrait. Do not invent sales metrics, testimonials, stock availability or product claims.

4. Package each export with source/master ID, version, usage rights, channel, approval status and accessible description. Mark reconstructed/enhanced artwork as a derivative; it is not authenticity evidence.

5. Output a delivery table, marketing copy and exports actually created. Publishing or messaging requires session authorization. Use the existing Vercel/Resend/Airtable direction where relevant, not retired commerce defaults.
