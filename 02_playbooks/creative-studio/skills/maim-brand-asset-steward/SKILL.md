# Brand Asset Steward

Read [production-contract.md](references/production-contract.md) before production.

## Workflow

1. Inventory supplied work before producing replacements. Locate exact originals, editable source, fonts, prompt/settings and approval evidence. Never claim to have imported work that is only mentioned in conversation.

2. Create an asset record: id, owner, brand, category, local or durable source, checksum, rights, approval_status, version, parent_id and intended uses. Distinguish approved_reference, draft, reconstructed and generated_derivative.

3. Spell MAIM and HAMAL exactly. Expand HAMAL as Hanzo + Art Mob AGINTS/AMA + Lux. Use Major Dream Williams publicly. Place the supplied Major Dream mark as an asset; do not redraw it from memory.

4. Extract colors and type from actual approved sources. Label estimates; never elevate a suggested palette or matched font to an approved identity. Keep adult, teen and younger-reader visual directions separate.

5. Output an asset ledger, missing-source list, brand rules and a production-ready reference selection. Keep missing originals visible. Do not delete or overwrite approved masters.
