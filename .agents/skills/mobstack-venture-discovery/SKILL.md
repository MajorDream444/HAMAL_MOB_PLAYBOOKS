# Mobstack Venture Discovery

## Capture intent and choose depth

- Record the project, decision owner, original wording, source/date, user, problem, desired outcome, and boundaries. For founder-led work preserve founder intent. For client-led work separate the client's words from the team's interpretation and flag what needs client confirmation.
- Choose the closest shape: app or commercial product, client engagement, workflow, utility, or campaign. Use deeper discovery for costly commitments, contested demand, or consequential risks. Focus small internal work on users, current process, and a testable outcome.
- Reflect back the intended outcome and separate approved requirements, proposals, and blocking questions. Ask only questions whose answers materially change the next decision. Continue useful research where possible.

## Pressure-test the idea

Apply these review lenses as needed. Keep evidence, interpretation, and simulation labeled separately.

1. **Market:** Identify direct competitors, substitutes, and the current workaround. For each material claim record the source, date, buyer, offer, pricing if observable, and evidence of the proposed gap. Compare who pays and why they switch. Treat a perceived gap as a hypothesis until tested.
2. **Buyer:** Simulate objections and buying scenarios to design better interviews. Separate these scenarios from observed behavior, customer quotes, pilots, or paid demand. Propose a real-world test with a pass criterion and record what would falsify the idea.
3. **Economics:** Where relevant, show the unit of sale, price, variable costs, contribution per unit, and the assumptions that matter most. Label inputs as observed, supplied, or hypothetical, and run downside and base cases with explicit currency and period. Leave unknowns visible.
4. **Feasibility:** Identify the smallest useful release, existing systems to preserve, critical dependencies, and the main failure path. Flag commerce, exclusive allocation, permissions, or sensitive data for applicable release checks.

Use these lenses directly. Delegate only when the active instructions authorize it. Research public evidence with sources when available. Keep private founder and client material in authorized locations. External interviews, outreach, and spending require separate authorization.

## Return a decision brief

Include the following in a reviewable brief:

- Owner and decision requested, with original intent and proposed scope
- Target user, current workaround, competitor evidence, and positioning hypothesis
- Observed buyer evidence, simulated objections, and the next validation test
- Relevant economics, assumptions, risks, and open decisions
- Recommendation to proceed to scope review, hold for evidence, redirect, or stop, with the next owner and action

Record human decisions with date and source. Treat approvals as scoped to the decision and version reviewed.

## Hand off approved scope

Use the workload's existing build-packet convention and the [Mobstack handoff](../../../02_playbooks/mobstack/build-handoff/README.md) where applicable. Map approved requirements to the PRD, app flow, design brief, technical requirements, backend schema, and implementation plan. Carry stable IDs from decisions to tickets and acceptance evidence. Mark missing inputs, reviewed inapplicability, and readiness explicitly. Keep build and release approvals separate.

For HAMAL Music Engine, scope intake, artist diagnostics, and future checkout separately. Route paid or exclusive offers to commerce staging checks for concurrent claims, payment retries, late payments, reconciliation, and audit history. Discovery ends with a decision brief or a scoped documentation handoff. Execute implementation or release only under separately authorized scope.
